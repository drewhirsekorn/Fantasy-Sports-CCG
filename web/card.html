Real data: this is card of the 48-card prototype pool, with its actual Form history and GameScores. Names are fictional on purpose — player likenesses need union agreements.

Rarity is a ladder, so it takes one hue stepped by lightness rather than five competing hues — the same ramp the collection grid uses, where having all five on screen at once forced the decision. Pip count carries the order without relying on colour at all, and Common goes unframed, because being untreated is what Common means.

Three things are encoded on this card and none of them wears another's colour: the rarity ladder in blue, the floor in gold, and Form — the number that matters most — in plain ink. The old palette failed that twice: its Signature was the same gold as the floor, so on a Signature card the tier and the floor were one colour.
